Privacy policy

This site is a plain set of pages about Westmont Sterling's commercial cleaning service in Phoenix. It collects as little as a website can.

Effective 7 November 2026. Westmont Sterling LLC, an Arizona limited liability company.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and loads nothing from other companies' servers.
  • It has no forms. Nothing you type on this site is sent anywhere.

What the hosting company may record

Like any web host, the company that serves these pages may keep standard server logs, such as the IP address, browser type, page requested and time of the request, to run and secure its service. We do not use those logs for advertising, and we do not combine them with anything else.

When you email or call us

If you email hello@westmontsterlingcommercial.com or call (619) 576-2390, we receive what you send: your name, contact details, property address, photos and message. We use it only to answer you, to quote and deliver the work you ask for, and to keep business and tax records.

To quote or do a job, we share the details of that job, such as the address, photos and access notes, with the company that will do the work. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone, and we do not add you to a mailing list without asking.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@westmontsterlingcommercial.com. We will keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
