Privacy

Privacy

What happens to what you type into our forms. Short version: we keep it to reply to you and to quote your sites, we never sell it, and this site does not track you.

Who we are

Westmont Sterling LLC, an Arizona company that sells commercial exterior cleaning in the Phoenix metro. Questions about this page go to hello@westmontsterlingcommercial.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the quote form or the call request form, or send us by email: for example your name, company, role, email, phone, your sites' addresses, the services you want, a deadline, an estimate and your notes. Each form also records the address of the page you sent it from, so we know which page you were reading. You choose what to give us; only your name and a way to reach you are needed.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored by our form host, Netlify, Inc., which keeps them so we can read them and reply. Emails you send us are kept in our mailbox.

How we use it

To reply to you, to prepare and send your quote, and, if you ticked the box that says so, to call or text you about your request. To price your sites we share the site details a local cleaning company needs to quote the work (the address area, the areas to clean and your photos), not your contact details unless a visit is booked. We never sell what you send us, and we do not use it for advertising.

No tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising or social-media trackers, no tracking cookies and no third-party fonts or scripts. The only request to another company happens when you press a form's send button, and it goes to our form host. Like any web host, the service that delivers these pages may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security; we do not use them to identify you.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to reply, quote and serve your sites, and to keep the business records the law requires. If you never become a customer, you can ask us to delete your request at any time.

Deleting or correcting it

Email hello@westmontsterlingcommercial.com from the address you used, or tell us the name and company you entered, and say what you want deleted or corrected. We confirm by email when it is done. More about how we work is on the about page and in the FAQ.
