Commercial pressure washing in Phoenix

Westmont Sterling sells commercial pressure washing for storefronts, restaurants, gas stations and medical offices in the City of Phoenix, Arizona. The work is done at your property by an established, insured Phoenix company working for us under our contract and our standard.

Booking status. We are choosing the Phoenix companies that will do the work now and have not yet done a job here. We will not book a visit until a vetted company is in place for your job. You can ask for a quote or a question at any time.

What is cleaned

  • Storefront sidewalks and entries
  • Dumpster pads and enclosures
  • Drive-thru lanes
  • Parking-lot flatwork
  • Building exteriors at ground level

Hot-water pressure washing with wash-water recovery or drain blocking on every job, to keep wash water out of Phoenix storm drains.

What you get

  • A written work order before anyone shows up
  • Time-stamped before-and-after photos of every area, sent the same day
  • A re-clean within 48 hours of any photo-proven miss, at no charge
  • One contact for scheduling, changes and complaints: Westmont, not the crew

Cleaning only

We sell cleaning and nothing else. Sealing, striping, painting and repairs are not sold, because in Arizona they can require a contractor licence and are taxed differently from cleaning. If you need them, we will tell you to look for a company licensed by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors, and that work goes on a separate contract.

Questions Phoenix buyers ask about commercial pressure washing

How much does commercial pressure washing cost in Phoenix?

Phoenix pressure-washing companies publish $0.15–$0.35 per square foot for sidewalks and flatwork, $75–$250 per dumpster pad and $150–$400 per drive-thru lane, with a $150 minimum (their own websites, November 2026). Your quote from us is one flat number per visit. Recurring plans are priced below one-off rates.

How do I get a quote?

Email photos of the areas, the address and how often you want the work done to hello@westmontsterlingcommercial.com. The company doing the work confirms the scope from the photos or a site visit, and we send one flat price per visit with a written work order.

Who actually does the work?

An established, insured Phoenix pressure-washing company does the work for Westmont under our contract. The crew tells you on site that it is working for Westmont. We hold the contract with you, set the standard and answer for the result.

What happens if an area is missed?

If the photos show an area on the work order was missed, the crew re-cleans it within 48 hours at no charge. Every area is photographed before and after, with a time stamp, so a miss is easy to show.

Do you offer monthly or quarterly plans?

Yes. A storefront plan can be monthly or quarterly, with the same crew, the same checklist and a fixed visit window. A one-off wash is available first if you want to try the service.

Where does the wash water go?

The crew must recover the wash water or block the drains on every job. The City of Phoenix requires businesses whose work could pollute runoff to keep a stormwater management plan under City Code Chapter 32C, and we check how each company handles water before it works for us.

Do you seal, stripe or paint?

No. We sell cleaning only. Sealing, striping, painting and repairs can require an Arizona contractor licence, so we point you to companies licensed by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors, on a separate contract.

When do I pay?

You pay before the first visit. Recurring plans are paid three days before each period. Today we invoice by ACH bank transfer; card payment is not available yet.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes. Phoenix pressure-washing companies publish minimum charges of about $150 (their own websites, November 2026), and small jobs are priced from that floor. Your quote states the full price per visit.

Which areas do you serve?

The City of Phoenix, Arizona. Mesa, Tempe, Scottsdale, Chandler, Glendale and other metro cities come later, after we check each city's own licence and stormwater rules.

When is the best time of year to pressure wash in Phoenix?

November through March is the main exterior-cleaning season in Phoenix because the weather is mild. Summer is harder on crews: Phoenix has about 111 days a year at or above 100°F (NOAA).

How long has Westmont Sterling been in business?

Since August 2026, when Westmont Sterling LLC was formed in Arizona. We have not yet done a job in Phoenix, and we say so. You can read about the company and its owner on the about page.
