Scope

What is included, and what is not

Every visit runs from a work order that lists the areas, the arrival window and the company doing the work. Your ticket can add lines; it never goes below these.

Included

  • Storefront sidewalks and entries: debris and gum gone, flatwork clean edge to edge with no stripes, clean under the entry mats, no standing water left at the door.
  • Dumpster pads and enclosures: pad, curb and walls free of grease film, gate fronts and bollards clean, bins back in place and the gate closed.
  • Drive-thru lanes: the lane, the curbs and the ground under the menu board and pickup window, scheduled outside your service hours.
  • Parking-lot flatwork and ground-level exteriors: cleaned without marking glass, signage or paint.
  • Wash water recovered on every job and never sent to the storm drain.

Not included

  • Sealing, striping or painting
  • Concrete or surface repairs
  • Anything that needs a contractor's licence

We do not sell these; when you need one, we tell you who is licensed for it.

Photo standard

What the photos must show

Before-and-after pairs from the same angle for every area on the checklist, at least four per visit and one per surface type, taken on site at the visit. Photos of the water recovery in place before spraying and of the collected water after.

The report reaches you the same day. You have one business day to flag a miss; a photo-shown miss is redone within 48 hours; if it is not, you do not pay for that visit, and anything already paid for it is refunded within five business days. The full list, area by area, is in our standard.

How you pay

The invoice comes only after the before-and-after photos have been in your inbox for one business day with nothing flagged. Larger visits and multi-site routes are paid by bank transfer before the visit, with the same photo window and the same redo-or-refund guarantee.

Month to month, 30 days' notice, no annual contract.

Short answers

Pressure washing questions

Can the work happen outside our opening hours?

Yes. Storefronts are usually washed before opening or after close, inside a one-hour arrival window on the work order. Drive-thru lanes are scheduled outside service hours or with a lane closure you agree to.

Where does the wash water go?

It is recovered on every job and never sent to the storm drain. The photos show the recovery set-up in place and the collected water afterwards.

Do you seal or stripe the lot?

No. We sell cleaning only. For sealing, striping, painting or repairs we point you to a licensed company.

How fast can I get a price?

Within two business days of your addresses and photos. If you are holding a notice with a date, say so on the quote form.

Who is the company on my site?

An established local pressure washing company with its own reviews and insurance, named on your work order before anyone arrives. More in the full FAQ.
