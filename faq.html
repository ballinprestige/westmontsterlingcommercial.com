Questions

Phoenix exterior cleaning questions, answered plainly

What franchise owners and property managers ask before they send us a site. If yours is not here, ask Kyle to call you.

Who does the work

Who actually does the work at my site?

An established local company in the trade, with its own reviews and its own insurance, named on your work order before anyone arrives. Westmont writes the work order, holds the crew to our written standard, checks every photo and stands behind the result. You deal with one contact: us.

Can I look the company up before the visit?

Yes. Its name is on your work order before anyone arrives, so you can check its reviews and its insurance yourself. The crew introduces itself by its own company name and shows a card with your job number on request.

What about insurance?

The certificate of insurance of the company doing your work is sent to you before the first visit, and names you as additional insured where your contract requires it. If your vendor file needs other documents, list them in the notes on the quote form and Kyle tells you what we can return.

Can you give me references?

The crew on your site is an established local company you can look up, named on your work order. The first site is the proof.

Payment and terms

How does payment work?

You pay after the photos: the invoice comes only after the before-and-after photos have been in your inbox for one business day with nothing flagged. Larger visits and multi-site routes are paid by bank transfer before the visit, with the same photo window and the same redo-or-refund guarantee. Each account gets one monthly statement that lists every visit and its charge.

What if something is missed?

Flag it from the photos inside the one-business-day window (a Friday-evening report stays open until Monday evening). A photo-shown miss is redone within 48 hours of your flag. If it is not, you do not pay for that visit, and anything already paid for it is refunded within five business days. After the window, silence counts as acceptance.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Plans run month to month and cancel on 30 days' notice. Twelve-month pricing is offered, never required. There is nothing to sign to get a price.

Can we start with one site?

Yes, and we suggest it. One location, one visit. Add the rest when the photos satisfy you.

Prices and timing

How fast is a quote?

Within two business days of your addresses and photos. Large flatwork may need the crew to see the site first; we tell you if so.

How are prices set?

For pressure washing in Phoenix we start from published Phoenix bands: sidewalks and flatwork $0.15–$0.35 per sq ft, dumpster pads $75–$250, drive-thru lanes $150–$400, with a $190 minimum call-out per site. Your real quote is one flat price per visit per site, from the local crew's price. Three or more sites are priced as a route, and recurring plans price below one-off visits. Window, solar and junk work is priced within two business days from your photos. Sales tax is added where the state taxes the service. Try the estimator.

I am holding a notice with a deadline. Can you help?

Tell us the date. Where a local crew has capacity we schedule inside the week, and the report is built to be forwarded to the city, the brand or the landlord: photos, scope, date and time, where the wash water went, and the crew's certificate of insurance. Same price, same photo window, nothing extra for the date.

Can you work around our opening hours?

Yes. Each visit has an arrival window on the work order, usually before opening or after close; drive-thru lanes are done outside service hours.

Where and what

Which cities do you cover?

Phoenix metro now. Tucson and Florida (Miami, Tampa Bay, Orlando) next: send us your sites anywhere in those regions.

What do you clean?

Pressure washing (storefront walks and entries, dumpster pads, drive-thru lanes, parking-lot flatwork, ground-level exteriors), storefront window cleaning, commercial solar-panel cleaning and commercial junk removal. Details are on the pressure washing page and in our standard.

What do you not sell?

Interior cleaning, sealing, striping, painting, repairs, coatings, electrical or inverter work, solar installation and hazardous-waste removal. When you need one of these, we tell you who is licensed for it.

What is the quarterly photo digest?

At no charge, every quarter, a digest across all your sites of what the photos show at each one and which trade handles it. It summarises the photos; it is not a condition or safety assessment.

How do I reach you?

Use the quote form, ask Kyle to call you, or email hello@westmontsterlingcommercial.com. Kyle Reynolds replies within one business day.
