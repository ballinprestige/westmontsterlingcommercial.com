Commercial cleaning in Phoenix, proven with photos

Westmont Sterling is an Arizona company. We sell commercial pressure washing to businesses in Phoenix under our own name. We write the contract and guarantee the result with photo proof. Established local companies do the work at your property under our contract.

What gets cleaned

  • Storefront sidewalks and entries
  • Dumpster pads and enclosures

Cleaning only. We do not sell sealing, striping, painting or repairs. Scope, plans and the price band are on the service page.

The photo-proof guarantee

Every area on your work order is photographed before and after, with a time stamp, and the photos are sent to you the same day.

If a photo shows an area was missed, the crew comes back and re-cleans it within 48 hours at no charge.

You have one contact for scheduling, changes and complaints: Westmont, not the crew.

Three steps to a quote
