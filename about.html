About Westmont Sterling: commercial cleaning for Phoenix

Westmont Sterling LLC is an Arizona company that sells and guarantees commercial exterior cleaning, and later janitorial, and pays vetted local companies to do the work. Our first city is Phoenix.

Who we are

Westmont Sterling LLC was formed in Arizona in August 2026. It is owned by Kyle Reynolds, who runs the company remotely. Westmont has no office in Phoenix that you can visit. The people at your property are the crew of the local company doing the work for us.

We are new. We have not yet done a job in Phoenix, we have no customer reviews, and we do not show testimonials or logos of clients we do not have. When that changes, this page will say so, and any review we show will be a real review copied from a real profile.

How a job works

One job, start to finish

  1. We sell and contract the job

    You buy from Westmont. We write the work order and hold the contract with you.

  2. A vetted local company does the work

    An established, insured Phoenix company does the cleaning at your property under our contract. Before it works for us, it sends proof of insurance, shows how it handles wash water and passes our checks.

  3. We check the photos and answer for the result

    Every area is photographed before and after. A photo-proven miss is re-cleaned within 48 hours at no charge, and you deal with us, not the crew.

What we never do

  • Claim jobs, clients, results or years in business that we do not have
  • Write, buy or ask friends and family for reviews
  • Hide which company is doing the work at your property
  • Sell sealing, striping, painting or repairs, which can require an Arizona contractor licence
  • Use photos that are not from our own jobs, or AI-made images presented as job photos
