About

Who stands behind your exterior cleaning in Phoenix

Westmont Sterling LLC is an Arizona company, owner-led by Kyle Reynolds. Westmont sells and guarantees commercial exterior cleaning, done by established local companies under our contract and our written standard.

What we do

One accountable name across all your sites

Kyle Reynolds owns and runs Westmont Sterling, which sells commercial exterior cleaning to businesses with several locations: franchise operators, plaza and strip-centre owners, gas-station operators, medical-office groups and small warehouse and office portfolios.

Every site gets one written standard: a work order before anyone arrives, a photo report the same day and one monthly statement. The company on your property is an established local company named on your work order, with its own reviews and insurance. Westmont writes the work order, checks the photos against our standard and answers for the result.

Cleaning and removal only. Repairs, coatings and anything that needs a contractor's licence are not sold; we point you to a licensed company. Our trades and prices for Phoenix are on the pressure washing page.

What we hold ourselves to

  • You pay after the photos, never before you have seen the work.
  • A photo-shown miss is redone within 48 hours. If it is not, you do not pay for that visit, and anything already paid is refunded within five business days.
  • The company on your site is named before it arrives.
  • No annual contract: month to month, 30 days' notice.
  • Start with one site.
