Westmont Sterling does not sell commercial janitorial in Phoenix yet. We are starting with commercial pressure washing in Phoenix. Janitorial comes after that service is running, and we have not set a date.

What it will be

Recurring cleaning inside commercial buildings in the City of Phoenix, such as offices, storefronts, restaurants and medical suites. The model will be the same as pressure washing. Westmont holds the contract with you and answers for the result. A vetted local janitorial company does the cleaning under our contract.

  • A written scope and checklist before service starts

Why there are no prices here
